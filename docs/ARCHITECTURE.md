# Lyth Architecture

## Everything feeds into Stories.

Lyth is a life operating system.

It is NOT a productivity app.
It is NOT a note taking app.
It is NOT a writing application.

Everything exists to help someone create, complete and remember the important Stories of their life.

---

↓

Stories

↓

Every object in the application ultimately belongs to a Story.

---

# Home

The Home screen answers one question.

"What should I do today?"

It should display:

- Current Story
- Today's Page
- Today's Tasks
- Recent Bookmark
- Weather
- Quick Actions

Home should never become a statistics dashboard.

---

# Stories

Stories are the heart of Lyth.

A Story is a meaningful life journey.

Examples

- 40 Before 40
- Run a Marathon
- Renovate My House
- Backpack Japan
- Learn Spanish
- Build a Business

A user may eventually have many Stories.

Only one Story is active at a time.

The active Story appears on Home.

---

# Story

A Story contains:

- Chapters
- Pages
- Collections
- Bookmarks
- Notes
- Photos
- AI Story Guide

The Story page is the central hub for everything related to that Story.

It displays:

Story details

Progress

Current Chapter

Today's Page

Collections

Bookmarks

Story Guide

---

# Chapters

A Story is divided into Chapters.

Chapters represent stages of a Story.

Examples

40 Before 40

Chapter 1

Building Foundations

Chapter 2

Creating Momentum

Chapter 3

Transformation

Chapter 4

Reflection

The number and names of Chapters are unique to each Story.

A Chapter contains many Pages.

---

# Pages

A Page represents one day.

Every day creates exactly one Page.

Pages contain:

Date

Tasks

Calendar events

Weather

Meals

Running

Yoga

Notes

Reflection

Photos

Bookmarks

Every Page belongs to one Chapter.

Every Chapter belongs to one Story.

---

# Tasks

Tasks help move the Story forward.

Tasks may be:

Generated by the Story Guide

Created manually

Imported from Apple Reminders

Completing Tasks contributes towards Story progress.

---

# Bookmarks

Bookmarks are memorable moments.

Examples

First 10K

Lost 1 Stone

Bought a House

Promotion

Amazing Sunset

Holiday

Bookmarking a moment tells the Story later.

Bookmarks can contain:

Title

Description

Date

Photos

Location

Bookmarks may be shared.

---

# Library

The Library contains Collections.

Collections are reusable.

Collections are not tied to one Story.

Instead they support many Stories.

---

Running

Running

---

# Story Guide

Every Story has one Story Guide.

The Story Guide is an AI companion.

It understands:

The Story

Progress

Pages

Tasks

Bookmarks

Goals

The Story Guide can:

Create Tasks

Answer questions

Suggest improvements

Encourage

Coach

The Story Guide only knows about the current Story.

---

# Story Teller

The Story Teller is only available when a Story is complete.

It analyses:

Pages

Photos

Bookmarks

Notes

Reflections

Progress

Collections

The Story Teller generates a beautiful written summary of the journey.

The output becomes a digital book.

---

# Bookshelf

Completed Stories move to the Bookshelf.

The Bookshelf is the user's life library.

Each completed Story becomes one book.

Opening a book displays:

Cover

Summary

Timeline

Bookmarks

Photos

Statistics

The user can:

Read it

Export it

Print it

Share it

The Bookshelf should feel like a real bookshelf.

# Design Principles

Everything feeds into Stories.

Pages represent days.

Stories represent journeys.

Bookmarks represent memories.

Collections provide tools.

The Story Guide helps during the journey.

The Story Teller writes the finished book.

Completed Stories live forever in the Bookshelf.

If a feature does not support a Story, it should not exist.
